Shikaku Brain Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Pawel Wedrowski ("we", "us") handles information in the Shikaku Brain game (the "Game"). Shikaku Brain is a logic puzzle game based on the classic Shikaku rules: divide a grid into rectangles so that each rectangle contains exactly one number, and that number equals the rectangle's area.

Information we collect

The Game does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, or other personal details in order to play.

Gameplay data such as progress, settings, and scores may be stored locally on your device.

We do not collect personal information from you unless you choose to contact us.

How information is used

Local gameplay data is used only to operate the Game on your device, for example to save progress and remember preferences.

If you email us, we use the information you send only to respond to your request.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties except if required by law.

Third-party services

The Game may use platform services provided by Apple, Google, or other app stores, for example to distribute the Game or process optional purchases. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Children

The Game is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention

Local game data remains on your device until you delete the Game or clear its data. If you contact us by email, we keep that correspondence only as long as needed to handle your request.

Your choices

  • You can stop using the Game at any time by uninstalling it.
  • You can delete locally stored progress by clearing the Game's data or uninstalling it.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change when we do.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy or Shikaku Brain can be sent to pawel.wedrowski@gmail.com.
